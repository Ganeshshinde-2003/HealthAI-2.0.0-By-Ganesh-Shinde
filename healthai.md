# Problem statement

## Core problem

Health-conscious users and health professionals struggle to turn fragmented health data into personalized, trustworthy, and actionable plans. Existing health workflows are slow, generic, hard to interpret, and weak at longitudinal behavior support. [page:0][web:5]

## Problem breakdown

- Health data is fragmented across lab reports, notes, symptom logs, apps, and habits. [page:0][web:5]  
- Users receive raw numbers or generic tips instead of meaningful interpretation and action. [page:0][web:5]  
- Recommendations often fail because they are not tied to behavior, consistency, or follow-up. [page:0]  
- Emotional and motivational friction is under-addressed in most health products; OviAI explicitly identifies minimal emotional support as a product gap. [page:0][file:11]  
- Health professionals spend too much time manually reviewing and summarizing data when structured AI assistance could reduce that burden. [cite:12][cite:13]

## Opportunity statement

If HealthAI can combine precise biomarker interpretation with adaptive behavior coaching, progress tracking, and trustworthy UX, it can become a daily/weekly health operating system instead of a one-time analysis tool. OviAI’s solution strategy centers on empathetic support, intuitive trust-building experiences, and a holistic ecosystem rather than a narrow tracker, which is a useful benchmark for HealthAI’s evolution. Below is the feature model if you want HealthAI to feel “like OviAI” in product depth, not just in UI polish.

---

## Pillar 1 — Structured health intelligence

This is your current strength. OviAI starts with structured tracking and trends instead of raw logging, and HealthAI should do the same for biomarkers and wellness data. [page:0]

### Must-have features

- Lab report upload and parsing  
- Biomarker extraction and normalization  
- Optimal vs clinical range analysis  
- Highlighted risk / deficiency / imbalance detection  
- Root cause pattern analysis  
- JSON output validation  
- Monthly trend comparison  
- Health summary generation  

These features align directly with your current product description of biomarker analysis, trend reports, and root cause analysis. [web:5]

### Why it matters

Without trusted structured interpretation, the rest of the product becomes a chatbot experience rather than a health intelligence system. [web:5]

---

## Pillar 2 — Personalized action engine

OviAI’s second major response is tailored lifestyle recommendations with small, evolving actions that reduce overwhelm. HealthAI needs this same layer, but built from biomarker + behavior context rather than cycle data alone. [page:0]

### Must-have features

- Personalized action plan after each analysis  
- Priority ranking: “focus on these 3 things first”  
- Recommendation categories:
  - Nutrition  
  - Sleep  
  - Movement  
  - Recovery  
  - Stress  
  - Supplements  
- Action difficulty score: easy / moderate / advanced  
- User goal modes: improve energy, weight, hormones, sleep, recovery, longevity  
- Recommendation explanation: why this action was recommended  
- Contraindication / caution messaging  
- Follow-up prompts after 7 / 14 / 30 days  

### Product rule

Do not overwhelm users with 15 recommendations. OviAI emphasizes small, actionable steps that evolve with progress, and HealthAI should follow that principle. [page:0]

---

## Pillar 3 — Behavior & habit layer

This is the biggest gap between a useful analyzer and a real startup product. OviAI explicitly uses behavior-led micro-goals, gentle nudges, and motivation rooted in reflection rather than aggressive gamification. [page:0]

### Must-have features

- Daily / weekly check-ins  
- Micro-habit goals tied to recommendations  
- Four Pillars habit scoring  
- Streaks or consistency tracking, but softly framed  
- Reflection prompts:
  - How was your sleep this week?  
  - Energy trend?  
  - Stress trend?  
  - What made it hard to stay consistent?  
- Habit nudges  
- Missed-goal recovery flows  
- “Do less, but more consistently” mode  

### Why this matters

This turns HealthAI from a diagnostic-feeling app into a longitudinal behavior companion. OviAI’s case study shows this layer is essential for trust, motivation, and long-term engagement. [page:0]

# User journey map

## Current journey

1. User gets lab reports or health concerns.  
2. User feels confused, anxious, or overwhelmed.  
3. User uploads files to HealthAI.  
4. HealthAI generates analysis and recommendations.  
5. User reads output.  
6. User may or may not act.  
7. User loses momentum because there is little structured follow-up.

## Target journey

1. User uploads lab reports and health context.  
2. HealthAI explains top findings in plain language.  
3. HealthAI prioritizes the top 3 actions.  
4. User picks a goal focus.  
5. HealthAI creates a weekly micro-plan.  
6. User checks in with habits / symptoms / energy.  
7. HealthAI updates insights based on adherence and trends.  
8. Monthly report reflects changes and adapts next steps.  
9. User feels supported, informed, and in control over time.

## FAQ Section

**Headline**
Frequently Asked Questions

### Is my health data secure?
Yes. We use HIPAA-compliant Google Cloud infrastructure with encrypted connections. We don't store your health data — it's processed in real-time and deleted immediately after analysis.

### What file formats do you accept?
We support PDF, Word (DOCX), Excel (XLSX), CSV, and plain text files. Upload lab reports, symptom logs, health assessments, or daily tracking data.

### How accurate is the AI analysis?
We use Google's Vertex AI (Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite) with prompts designed by functional medicine experts. While highly accurate, this is for informational purposes only — always consult a healthcare provider for medical decisions.

### What biomarkers do you analyze?
We analyze 50+ biomarkers including complete blood count, comprehensive metabolic panel, lipid panel, thyroid hormones, sex hormones, cortisol, inflammatory markers, vitamins, and more.

### Can I track progress over time?
Yes! Upload new lab reports monthly and we'll generate trend analysis with radar charts, hormonal insights, and behavior pattern tracking.

### Do you replace my doctor?
No. Nova Health is a tool to help you understand your health data better. Always work with qualified healthcare providers for diagnosis and treatment.

### How long does analysis take?
Most analyses complete in 30-60 seconds. More complex reports with multiple files may take up to 2 minutes.

### Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel your subscription anytime with one click. No contracts, no commitments.

# Landing Page Design Guidelines

## Color Palette

**Primary Colors**
- Primary: #667eea (Purple-blue gradient start)
- Primary Dark: #764ba2 (Purple gradient end)
- Accent: #10b981 (Green for success/optimal)

**Secondary Colors**
- Warning: #f59e0b (Yellow for "keep in mind")
- Danger: #ef4444 (Red for "needs attention")
- Info: #3b82f6 (Blue for informational)

**Neutral Colors**
- Gray 900: #111827 (Headings)
- Gray 700: #374151 (Body text)
- Gray 500: #6b7280 (Secondary text)
- Gray 200: #e5e7eb (Borders)
- Gray 50: #f9fafb (Backgrounds)
- White: #ffffff

## Typography

**Headings**
- Font: Inter, system-ui, sans-serif
- H1: 48px / 3rem, bold (Hero)
- H2: 36px / 2.25rem, bold (Sections)
- H3: 24px / 1.5rem, semibold (Subsections)
- H4: 20px / 1.25rem, semibold (Cards)

**Body**
- Font: Inter, system-ui, sans-serif
- Large: 18px / 1.125rem (Subheadlines)
- Regular: 16px / 1rem (Body text)
- Small: 14px / 0.875rem (Captions)

## Visual Style

**Cards**
- Border radius: 12px
- Shadow: 0 4px 20px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)
- Padding: 24px / 1.5rem
- Border: 1px solid #e5e7eb

**Buttons**
- Primary: Gradient #667eea → #764ba2
- Border radius: 8px
- Padding: 12px 32px
- Shadow on hover: 0 6px 30px rgba(102, 126, 234, 0.4)

**Spacing**
- Section padding: 80px top/bottom (desktop), 48px (mobile)
- Container max-width: 1280px
- Grid gap: 24px

**Animations**
- Fade in on scroll
- Smooth hover transitions (0.3s ease)
- Gradient animations for CTAs
- Slide up for cards

## Tone & Voice

**Characteristics**
- Trustworthy and professional
- Warm and supportive (not clinical)
- Clear and jargon-free
- Evidence-based but accessible
- Empowering, not fear-based

**Do's**
- Use "you" and "your" (personal)
- Explain "why" behind features
- Show empathy for user pain points
- Include disclaimers where appropriate
- Use concrete examples

**Don'ts**
- Medical jargon without explanation
- Diagnostic or prescriptive language
- Fear-mongering or health anxiety
- Over-promising results
- Comparing to doctors/medical care
