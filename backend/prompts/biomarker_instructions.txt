--- Specific Instructions for Biomarker Analysis ---

• Only include the "cycle_impact" field for biomarkers that have a real, direct impact on menstrual cycles, AND only if the user has provided menstrual cycle data. This includes sex hormones (Estradiol, Progesterone), gonadotropins (LH, FSH), and certain thyroid markers when cycle tracking is mentioned.
• Do NOT include the "cycle_impact" field for biomarkers without cycle relevance (e.g., Vitamin D, Cholesterol, Glucose) OR if no cycle data was provided by the user.
• For the "why_it_matters" field, write a thorough, descriptive explanation (at least 4-5 sentences) using real-life analogies and explicit connections to the user's health context, symptoms, and personal data provided.
